# Employee Leave Handbook

## 1. Introduction

This handbook describes the leave arrangements of Brightwater Software Ltd. for all employees based in Dublin. It is maintained by the People team and reviewed every year. The Leave Assistant applies the rules described here; when in doubt, contact people@brightwater.example.

## 2. Annual leave

Every employee is entitled to twenty-five (25) working days of annual leave per calendar year. In the year of hire the entitlement is pro-rated by months of employment. The entitlement is credited on 1 January.

### 2.1 Carry-over

Up to five unused days carry over into the next calendar year and must be taken by 31 March. Days not taken by then expire.

### 2.2 Requesting leave

Submit requests at least three working days in advance through the Leave Assistant. Requests of up to five working days are approved by your manager; longer requests are also approved by the People team.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees have ten days of paid sick leave per year. Absences of up to three days are self-declared. From the fourth day a medical certificate is required and reviewed by the People team.

## 4. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be granted by your manager and the People team once annual leave is exhausted.

## 5. Public holidays

Irish public holidays are listed in the document "Public Holidays 2026" and are not deducted from the annual leave balance.
