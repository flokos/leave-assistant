# Sick Leave Procedure

## Step 1: Tell your manager on the first day

Let your manager know by 09:30 on the first day of absence, by phone or through the Leave Assistant. Sick leave of up to three days is acknowledged automatically without a certificate.

## Step 2: Medical certificate

For absences longer than three days a medical certificate is required. Attach it to the request within five working days of your return. Only the People team can see the certificate.

## Step 3: Review by the People team

The People team checks the certificate and approves the leave. If the sick leave balance is exhausted, the case is handled under the Exceptional Leave Guidelines.

## Step 4: Reporting late

Sick leave can be reported up to thirty days after the first day of absence. Later reports need the approval of the People team.
