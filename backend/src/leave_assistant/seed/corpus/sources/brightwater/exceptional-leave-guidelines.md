# Exceptional Leave Guidelines

Internal document of the People team. It describes how cases outside the Employee Leave Handbook are handled. It is not shared with employees or managers.

## 1. Negative sick leave balance

When an employee exhausts the ten days of sick leave, the People team may approve up to five additional days with a medical certificate. The approval is recorded as a balance adjustment with a reason.

## 2. Compassionate leave

The People team may grant up to five days of paid compassionate leave after discussing the circumstances with the manager. Compassionate leave is recorded as an adjustment and does not reduce annual leave.

## 3. Requests without notice

Annual leave requests with less than three working days' notice are approved by the People team only for urgent family reasons. The manager is informed before the approval.

## 4. Unpaid leave above twenty days

Unpaid leave longer than twenty working days requires the agreement of the Managing Director. The People team prepares a note on the impact on the staffing plan.

## 5. Carry-over above five days

Employees who could not take leave because of a release commitment may carry over up to ten days with the approval of the Head of Engineering. The exception is valid for one year.

## 6. Record keeping

Every exception is recorded in the audit log with the approver, the date and the reason.
