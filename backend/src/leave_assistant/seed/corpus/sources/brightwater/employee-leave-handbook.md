# Employee Leave Handbook

## 1. Introduction

This handbook describes the leave arrangements of Brightwater Software Ltd. for all employees based in Dublin. It is maintained by the People team and reviewed every year. The Leave Assistant applies the rules described here; when in doubt, contact people@brightwater.example. This edition replaces the previous handbook.

## 2. Annual leave

Every employee is entitled to twenty (20) working days of annual leave per calendar year. In the year of hire the entitlement is pro-rated by months of employment and rounded to the nearest half day. The entitlement is credited on 1 January.

### 2.1 Carry-over

Up to five (5) unused days carry over into the next calendar year and must be taken by 31 March. Days not taken by then expire. Balances above five days are not carried over.

### 2.2 Requesting leave

Submit requests at least three (3) working days in advance through the Leave Assistant. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as working days. Requests of up to five (5) working days are approved by your manager; longer requests are approved by your manager and then by the People team.

### 2.3 Changing or cancelling approved leave

You can change or cancel approved leave before it starts. A change goes through approval again. Leave that has already started can only be cancelled by the People team.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees have ten (10) days of paid sick leave per year. Absences of up to three (3) days are self-declared and acknowledged automatically. From the fourth day a medical certificate is required; attach it to the request and the People team will review it. Sick leave can be reported up to thirty (30) days after the first day of absence. Certificates are visible to the People team only.

## 4. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be granted once annual leave is exhausted or for serious personal reasons. It always requires the approval of your manager and of the People team and does not reduce your annual leave balance.

## 5. Public holidays

Irish public holidays are listed in the document "Public Holidays 2026". Holidays falling on working days are not deducted from the annual leave balance.
