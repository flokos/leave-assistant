# Leave Policy (English translation)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Leave Policy of Aegean Logistics S.A. defines the leave types, employee entitlements and the request and approval procedure. It applies to all full-time employees in the Piraeus offices and the warehouses. Requests are submitted through the Leave Assistant or to the Human Resources department. In case of doubt the Greek text (Κανονισμός Αδειών) prevails.

## 2. Leave types

| Leave type | Annual entitlement | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 20 working days | Manager, plus HR for more than 5 days |
| Sick leave | 10 days | Automatic acknowledgement up to 3 days, HR above 3 days |
| Unpaid leave | Case by case | Manager and HR |

## 3. Annual leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Every employee is entitled to twenty (20) working days of annual leave per calendar year. In the year of hire the entitlement is pro-rated by months of employment and rounded to the nearest half day. The entitlement is credited on 1 January of each year.

### 3.2 Carry-over

Up to five (5) unused days of annual leave carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they expire. Any balance above five days is not carried over.

### 3.3 Request and notice

An annual leave request is submitted at least three (3) working days before it starts. The employee states the start and end dates; public holidays and weekends do not count as working days. Requests overlapping another pending or approved leave are rejected.

### 3.4 Approval

Requests of up to five (5) working days are approved by the department manager. Requests for more than five working days require the manager's approval followed by the approval of Human Resources. The employee is informed of every decision through the Leave Assistant.

### 3.5 Modification and cancellation

Approved leave can be modified or cancelled by the employee before it starts. Modifying approved leave goes through the approval procedure again. Leave that has already started can only be cancelled by Human Resources.

## 4. Sick leave

Every employee has ten (10) days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of up to three (3) days is declared without a certificate and acknowledged automatically. Sick leave longer than three days requires a medical certificate, attached to the request and checked by Human Resources. Sick leave can be declared retroactively up to thirty (30) days after the first day of absence. Medical certificates are accessible to Human Resources only.

## 5. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave is granted case by case, when annual leave is exhausted or for serious personal reasons. The request requires the approval of the manager and of Human Resources regardless of its length. Unpaid leave does not reduce the annual leave balance.

## 6. Public holidays

The official public holidays of the year are published in the document "Αργίες 2026". Holidays that fall on working days are not deducted from the annual leave balance.

## 7. Contact

Questions about this policy go to Human Resources (hr@aegean-logistics.example). The policy is reviewed every January.
