ENGINEERING RELEASE FREEZE DATES 2026

Notice for the Engineering department. During a release freeze no annual leave is approved for engineers on the release rota unless the Head of Engineering agrees.

Spring release freeze: 9 March 2026 to 20 March 2026.

Summer release freeze: 15 June 2026 to 26 June 2026.

Autumn release freeze: 21 September 2026 to 2 October 2026.

Winter release freeze: 7 December 2026 to 18 December 2026.

Requests that overlap a freeze period must be submitted at least ten working days before the freeze starts. Questions go to the Head of Engineering.
