# Employee Leave Handbook

Every employee is entitled to twenty working days of annual leave per calendar year, pro-rated in the hire year.
Requests must be submitted at least three working days before the leave starts. Please follow the instructions in
section 3 when you submit a request; the manager approves requests of up to five days and HR approves longer ones.

Up to five unused days carry over into the next year and expire on 31 March. Contact people@brightwater.example
or visit https://intranet.brightwater.example/leave for the form.
